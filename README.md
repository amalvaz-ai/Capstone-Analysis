Data Analytics Capstone Project (QM 640)
The Project analyses EV Car Sales effect on Gas Powered Car Sales and specifically the influence of EV Adoption with economic determinents 
such as the policy incentives and gasoline price. 

The data extracts for this analysis are from goverment data sources:

(1)EV vs Gas Cars registration in US States from 2016 to 2023
(2)Gas price history in US by region 
(3)EV Laws and Incentives in US by states

Research Questions:
Do increases in EV sales reduce gas-powered vehicle sales volumes?
How effective are government incentives in driving EV adoption?
What is the relationship between gasoline prices and EV sales?
How will EV and gas vehicle sales evolve in future years?


Analytical Techniques:

Linear regression models to test substitution and policy effects
ANOVA to find policy impact to EV adoption
Pearson correlation and hypothesis testing (α = 0.05)
Power analysis to validate statistical reliability
ARIMA time-series models to forecast EV and gas vehicle sales

Key Findings

Results suggest partial substitution, indicating an ongoing but incomplete transition away from ICE vehicles.
EV sales show sustained growth, while gas vehicle sales exhibit stagnation or gradual decline in several states.
Gasoline prices have a statistically significant relationship with EV adoption.
Policy groups have varying EV adoption rates 
Forecasts project continued EV growth with a gradual decline in gas vehicle sales over time.
